Backgammon Parlor

A game of backgammon at a real table, on iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV.

Play the computer, a second player on the same device, or someone online. A lobby shows who has the app open. You can challenge them, add them as a friend, and chat. The music is recorded classical pieces, not a synthesized jingle. There are no ads, and nothing in the game is locked behind a purchase.
