Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy describes what Backgammon Parlor, made by David Brooks, collects and stores when you use the game on iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV. It is written from what the app actually does. If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the date above.

A short summary

There are no ads, no tracking, and no analytics or crash-reporting kit in the app. To play online we use Apple’s Game Center and iCloud. The lobby keeps a short record of who is online, lobby chat, challenges, and friend requests. Optional feedback can include an email if you type one. Optional tips, when available, are processed by Apple. Settings and your block list stay on your device.

Information we collect

Game Center

Online play uses your Game Center account. We read your Game Center player ID and display name so other players can see who you are, and so presence, chat, challenges, friend requests, reports, and matchmaking can work. Your first name may appear next to your photo in the game.

If you have a Game Center profile photo, the app loads a small copy and shows it in the lobby, in chat, and on the board. Initials are shown when there is no photo. Photos are kept in memory on your device while the app is open. We do not upload them to our iCloud database or to any server of our own.

Matchmaking, invites, and live games run through Game Center. In-game chat during a live match is sent over that match to the other player. It is not written to our iCloud database.

Apple provides Game Center. Apple’s own privacy policy covers your Game Center account.

The lobby (iCloud)

The lobby uses the app’s iCloud container (iCloud.com.davidabrooks.backgammon), in the public database. Reading who is online can work without iCloud. To list yourself, chat, challenge someone, or send a friend request, you need an iCloud account on the device. We store:

  • Presence. Your Game Center player ID, display name, the last time you were seen, and whether you are in a game. The app updates this about every 20 seconds while it is open, and deletes it when you leave. Anyone not seen for about a minute is hidden from the list.
  • Lobby chat. Your player ID, display name, the message (up to 140 characters), and the time. The app shows messages from the last 30 minutes, up to the 100 newest. Older messages are not shown, but the app does not automatically delete them from iCloud.
  • Challenges. Who sent the challenge, their name, who it was sent to, and the time. A challenge is treated as fresh for about a minute.
  • Friend requests and replies. Who asked, their name, who it was sent to, whether it was accepted or declined, and the time. These records stay so the app can show who is friends and honor a one-week wait after a decline before the same person can ask again.
  • Reports. If you report someone in the lobby, we store your player ID, the reported player’s ID and name, any message you reported, and the reason you gave. Only the developer can read reports.
  • Bans. If we remove a player from the lobby, a ban record (their player ID, and a note we write) is stored so the app can hide them and drop their messages for everyone.

Feedback

On iPhone and iPad, Tip Jar › Send Feedback can save a message, an optional email address if you type one, the app version, the device type and system version, and the date. The email is used only to reply. Feedback is stored in the same iCloud container. Only the developer can read it. There is no Send Feedback form on Apple TV.

Tips (when available)

Optional one-time and monthly tips, when they are offered in the Tip Jar, are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your card number. Apple may tell the app whether a monthly tip is active, so it can show that in the Tip Jar. The Tip Jar is on iPhone and iPad only.

What stays on your device

Settings — including your room and board, sound, music, ambient volume, language, and whether you agreed to the lobby rules — are stored on the device. The list of players you have blocked is also stored only on that device. It is not synced through iCloud or Game Center. Deleting the app removes this on-device copy.

What we do not collect

The app does not include an advertising SDK, an analytics kit, or a crash reporter. It does not use tracking, and it does not show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. It does not have its own account system. It does not collect precise location, health, contacts from your address book, or financial information beyond what Apple already holds for an App Store purchase.

The lobby does not load or store your Game Center friends list. “Invite a Friend” uses Apple’s Game Center screen. (When the app looks up a profile photo, it may ask Game Center about that one player.)

How we use this information

We use it to run the game: to show who is online, start matches, deliver chat and friend requests, display names and photos, review reports, and, if you ask, reply to feedback. We do not sell this information. We do not use it for advertising or tracking.

How long we keep it

  • Presence is deleted when you leave the app’s foreground path, and stale names drop off the list after about a minute. A leftover presence record may remain until it is overwritten or deleted.
  • Lobby chat is shown only for the last 30 minutes. Records may remain in iCloud until we delete them or you ask us to.
  • Challenge records may remain after they expire on screen, until they are deleted.
  • Friend requests and replies are kept so friendships and the decline cooldown still work.
  • Reports and feedback are kept so we can review them and act if needed.
  • In-game chat is not stored in our iCloud database.
  • Game Center photos are not saved by us after the app is closed.

Deleting your information

To ask us to delete the lobby data we hold about you — presence, chat messages, challenges, friend requests and replies, and any feedback or reports you sent — email dabrux@icloud.com. Please include your Game Center display name if you can. We will delete the records we control in the app’s iCloud container.

We cannot delete your Game Center account, your Game Center profile, or App Store purchase history. Those are held by Apple. You can manage or delete your Apple Account in Settings, or at privacy.apple.com.

Uninstalling the app removes the settings and block list stored on that device. It does not by itself remove lobby records in iCloud.

Children

Backgammon Parlor is not designed for children under 13, and it is not submitted as a Made for Kids app. The lobby lets you chat and play with people you may not know, which is why we expect an App Store age rating of 12 or older. If you are a parent or guardian and think we have personal information from a child under 13, write to us and we will delete it.

Sharing

Other people in the lobby can see your Game Center name, whether you are in a game, your photo or initials, and any lobby messages or challenges you send. Apple hosts Game Center, iCloud, and App Store purchases. We do not share this information with advertising companies or other analytics services, because the app does not use them.

If we are required by law to disclose information, we will do so only as required.
