Support

Short answers about playing online, the lobby, chat, and settings. If these don’t help, write to dabrux@icloud.com.

How do I play?

From the title screen, choose vs Computer, 2 Players (two people on the same device), or Play Online. Help on the title screen covers the rules as this app plays them, and how to move checkers. There is no doubling cube; the cube stays at 1, including in online games.

How do I play online?

Sign in to Game Center, then open Play Online or the lobby. You can:

  • Open the lobby, find someone who is online, and tap Play. They can Accept or Decline. When they accept, the match starts.
  • Start a game with the next available player.
  • Invite a Game Center friend.

Online games are live matches, not correspondence. You need a Game Center account. To appear in the lobby, chat, send a challenge, or send a friend request, you also need to be signed in to iCloud on the device.

What is the lobby?

The lobby lists people who have the app open right now. Someone already in a match is marked “In a game.” You stay listed while the app is in the foreground — on the title screen, in the lobby, or in a game. If you close the app or leave it in the background, you drop off the list after about a minute.

Friends you’ve accepted in the app are pinned at the top of the list with a Friend badge. A blocked player is hidden.

How do I add a friend?

On iPhone and iPad, tap Add Friend on a player’s card in the lobby, in the player panel during a live game, or on the end-of-game screen. They get a request in their lobby and can Accept or Decline. Once they accept, they are pinned at the top of your online list. If they decline, you have to wait a week before asking again.

On Apple TV, Add Friend is available in the lobby. This in-app friends list is separate from Game Center friends. You can still invite a Game Center friend to play through Play Online.

How does chat work?

On iPhone and iPad you can chat in the lobby and during a live game. Messages are limited to 140 characters. A short word filter is applied when you send and when a message is shown, and sending is rate-limited so the chat can’t be flooded.

The first time you use lobby chat, you are asked to agree to a short rule: be kind; no offensive, hateful, or sexual messages, and no harassment. There is zero tolerance for abuse.

Apple TV can list who is online and take challenges, but it has no lobby chat and no in-game chat.

How do I report or block someone?

In the lobby, open the menu (•••) next to a name, or press and hold a message, then choose Report or Block. A lobby report is sent to us so we can review it and, if needed, remove that player from the lobby.

During a live game, Report opens that player’s Game Center profile so you can report them to Apple (tap More, then Report). You can also report from the Games app. Block hides that person’s messages, presence, and challenges on this device. In a live game, you can also Block and Resign.

Block is stored on this device only. It does not sync to your other devices, and the app does not yet offer a way to unblock a single person. If you need a block lifted, write to us.

How do I sign in to Game Center?

On iPhone or iPad, go to the Settings app, then Game Center, and sign in with your Apple Account. On Apple TV, sign in under Game Center in Settings. You can also start Play Online in the app; if you aren’t signed in, Game Center’s sign-in screen is offered then. The lobby will ask you to sign in if you aren’t already.

How do I change music and sound?

Open Settings from the gear on the title screen. There are three controls:

  • Sound turns the game’s sound effects on or off. Sound follows the device’s silent switch.
  • Music plays recorded pieces: Albinoni on the title screen, Mozart when you win, and Chopin when you lose. Music is available only when Sound is on.
  • Ambient Sound, with a volume control, is the room: a fire in the Parlor, waves at the Beach, the street on City Night, and a solo-piano playlist in the Living Room.

You can turn any of these off. Recording credits are listed at the bottom of Settings.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings in the app and choose Language. You can use the system default, or pick English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Türkçe, Português (Brasil), 日本語, Русский, 简体中文, Bahasa Indonesia, or 繁體中文（香港）. The app’s text switches right away. On iPhone and iPad you can also set a per-app language in the iOS Settings app.

How does Apple TV work?

The same game is on Apple TV (included with the iPhone and iPad purchase). After you roll, focus the board, use the Siri Remote arrows to move among playable checkers, click to lift, nudge with the arrows, and click again to drop. An illegal drop snaps back. The title-screen Help page covers this in more detail.

The lobby on Apple TV shows who is online and lets you send or accept a challenge. There is no lobby chat, no in-game chat, and no Tip Jar on the TV app.

Tips and feedback

On iPhone and iPad, the heart on the title screen opens the Tip Jar. Tips are optional and unlock nothing. If monthly tips are available, you can cancel them any time under Tip Jar › Manage Subscriptions, or in your Apple ID subscription settings. The Tip Jar also has a Send Feedback form. You can leave an email if you’d like a reply.

For billing questions about a tip, you can also write to us or use Apple’s purchase support, since Apple processes the payment.
