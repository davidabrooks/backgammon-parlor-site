On iPhone and iPad, you can chat in the lobby and during a live game. The first time you use lobby chat, you must agree to a short in-app rule. That rule, and these terms, require the following:

  • Be kind.
  • Do not send offensive, hateful, or sexual messages.
  • Do not harass, threaten, or impersonate anyone.
  • Do not try to flood, spam, or disrupt the lobby or a game.

There is zero tolerance for this kind of abuse. Messages are limited to 140 characters, and a short word filter is applied, but a filter is not a complete defense. We may hide or remove messages, and we may ban a player from the lobby. A ban can hide that player and drop their messages for everyone.

You can Report or Block someone from the lobby menus, and you can report an opponent in a live game through Game Center. Block works on the device where you set it. If you see abuse, please report it, or write to dabrux@icloud.com.

You are responsible for the messages you send. The lobby includes people you may not know.
